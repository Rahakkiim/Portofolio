About the Project

This project is an interactive dashboard that analyzes the mental health of healthcare workers. It visualizes data from 5,000 employees, covering stress levels, job satisfaction, and burnout frequency. The dashboard helps identify key stress factors, such as heavy workload and poor work environment. It also displays job satisfaction by department and employee type, along with turnover intention and access to employee assistance programs (EAPs).

Main Features

  • Displays the total number of employees, average job satisfaction level, and average stress level.
  • Visualizes Burnout Frequency to show how often employees experience work exhaustion.
  • Analyzes key stress factors such as Heavy Workload, Poor Work Environment, and Career Stagnation..
  • Shows the distribution of stress levels based on the number of employees.
  • Compares job satisfaction across different departments and job types.
